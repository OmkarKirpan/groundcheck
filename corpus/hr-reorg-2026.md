# Support Reorganisation 2026

Confidential. Visible to HR administrators only.

## Change

The Pune support team will merge into the Customer Success organisation on 1 January 2027. Twelve Support roles are affected, including the two team lead positions.

## Timeline

The reorganisation will be announced to staff on 16 November 2026. Affected employees will meet HR individually in the week after the announcement.
