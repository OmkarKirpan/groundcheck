# Benefits FAQ

## Health insurance

All employees are covered by the company health plan from their first day. Partners and children can be added at no extra cost.

## Pension

Halvicor matches pension contributions up to 5 percent of base salary.

## Gym

Employees get a gym subsidy of EUR 30 per month.
