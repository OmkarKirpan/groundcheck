# Information Security Policy

## Accounts

Multi-factor authentication is required on every company account. Passwords must be at least 14 characters long.

## Devices

Laptops lock automatically after 5 minutes without activity. Report a lost or stolen laptop to the security team within 1 hour.

## Data

Customer data must never be copied to personal devices or personal cloud storage.
