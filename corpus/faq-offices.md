# Offices FAQ

## Where are the offices?

Halvicor Systems has offices in Lisbon and Pune. The head office is in Lisbon.

## When is the Lisbon office open?

The Lisbon office is open from 07:30 to 20:00 on working days.

## How do visitors get in?

Visitors sign in at reception and get a day badge. The host must meet them at reception.
