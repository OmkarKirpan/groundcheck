# Halvicor Relay Overview

Halvicor Relay is a fleet telematics platform. It tracks vehicles, trailers and equipment in real time.

## Limits

One Relay account supports up to 50,000 devices.

## Data retention

Relay keeps location history for 13 months. Older data is deleted automatically.
